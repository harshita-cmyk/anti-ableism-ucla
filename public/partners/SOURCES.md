# Partner logo sources

Retrieved 2026-11-10. Logos retain their source geometry, colors, and aspect ratios. No recoloring, tracing, or fabricated artwork. The Foundation SVG is extracted from the official header with its computed CSS fill colors embedded for standalone rendering.

The existing Anti-Ableism archive at https://www.anti-ableism.org/partners displays the Select Physical Therapy, Disability Rights California, StarVista, and Zhu assets reused here. This records existing first-party usage, not a new trademark license or blanket permission. LightHouse, Vista Center, and Foundation Fighting Blindness assets come directly from their official websites. No express external reuse grant was established; confirm any partner-specific publication permissions through the organization.

Select Physical Therapy is the original 181 × 77 transparent PNG; rendering is capped at its natural width. Zhu is 297 × 89, and StarVista is 1130 × 699. Higher-resolution Select artwork would improve high-density displays.
