## Platform

## Users

Students, researchers, and professionals interested in joining the organization or collaborating on accessibility projects. The website also serves the disabled community, people following the organization's initiatives and progress, and institutional stakeholders evaluating relevant work.

## Product Purpose

Anti-Ableism at UCLA is a student association promoting campus accessibility through advocacy, activism, legislation, and education. Its work concerns accessibility for students and faculty.

The website has two main purposes:

1. Attract students, researchers, and professionals who want to join the organization or collaborate on accessibility projects. Introduce the organization, its mission, and its members, and make opportunities to get involved clear.
2. Serve as a public archive of past and ongoing accessibility initiatives so visitors can understand project goals, progress, outcomes, and impact over time.

Accessibility is a foundational requirement because the website serves the disabled community. Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA across navigation, content, interactions, and media, including keyboard accessibility, screen-reader compatibility, clear navigation, sufficient contrast, semantic HTML and heading structure, visible focus indicators, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
